On 18 February 2014, Damascus held its first Startup Weekend.

It ran for 54 hours over three days, with a bootcamp for participants beforehand and international entrepreneurs joining online. The winner got access to venture funding and publicity. For a city in the middle of a war, it was the first real look the outside world got at the entrepreneurial community here.
