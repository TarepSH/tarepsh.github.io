The World Robot Olympiad is a global robotics competition for young people, built around Lego Mindstorms from LEGO Education. It started in Singapore in 2004 and now draws more than 26,000 teams from over 65 countries.

I co-organized the national competition for Syria: more than 75 teams, 25 judges, 30 volunteers and 400 robot matches.

The competition was sponsored by the [Syrian Computer Society](http://www.scs.org.sy/) and co-organized with [Wassem Saad](https://www.linkedin.com/in/waseem-saad-1924a043/).
