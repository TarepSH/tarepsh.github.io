## What is WRO?

The World Robot Olympiad is a global robotics competition for young people, built around Lego Mindstorms from LEGO Education. It started in Singapore in 2004 and now draws more than 26,000 teams from over 65 countries.

The Open Category is project-based. Teams build their own robotics solution around the theme of the season and present it to a group of judges on competition day.

## Latakia 2030

Latakia 2030 is our picture of what Syria could be by 2030: a country that runs on current technology — green building, clean energy, data mining, image processing, virtual and augmented reality, machine learning and computer vision.

We chose Latakia as the place to start because of its history and its geography. It has the sea, the mountains and a living city, all in one place.
