I automated the collection, processing, analysis, visualisation and sharing of data from more than 100 warehouses, covering over a million distributed items for more than 3.5 million people in Syria. The pipeline was built with Dataiku and Python.

Some of the code is public: [Dataiku auto warehousing and distribution](https://github.com/Syrian-Arab-Red-Crescent-SARC/Dataiku-auto-warehousing-and-distribution/blob/master/recipes/compute_test.py).
