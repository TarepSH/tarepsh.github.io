The IFRC collects data from 191 National Societies through its Federation-wide Databank and Reporting System (FDRS). Checking that data by hand does not scale, so we use AI and open-source tools to validate it.

I presented this session at the IFRC Global Innovation Summit 2023: what we automate today, how the tools work, and a live demonstration — with data privacy and protection as the constraint throughout.
