Spike is an online platform that makes AI usable for businesses, governments and other organisations.

Most organisations need AI but cannot build and maintain it in-house. Spike uses their own data to support the people already doing the work, not to replace them.

It provides three things:

1. A data warehouse
2. Interactive analytics
3. Prediction and forecasting reports

And it asks for very little in return:

- No AI or machine learning experience needed
- No infrastructure to set up
- Built for the MENA region, and cheaper than the alternatives

Spike was also my graduation project for the Bachelor of Business Administration, in the Management Information Technology department at the Arab International University.
